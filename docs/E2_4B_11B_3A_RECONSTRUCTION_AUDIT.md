## Interpretation

This experiment establishes:

1. Agreement between the validated forward integrator and
   the full-space constraint formulation.
2. Successful nonlinear SQP reconstruction from a small
   deterministic perturbation.
3. Mass conservation to near floating-point precision.
4. Agreement between independent rank calculations and
   SQP iteration diagnostics.
5. A baseline against which subsequent unknown-initial-state
   experiments can be compared.

It does not establish:

- Reconstruction from an independently constructed initial estimate.
- Identifiability of an unknown initial state.
- Robustness to measurement noise.
- Predictive uncertainty decay.
- Engineering decision reliability.

## Research Objective

> The purpose of our research is not simply to reconstruct a pipeline
> state. It is to determine how much measurement information is needed,
> how rapidly uncertainty loses its influence on future behavior, and
> whether the resulting predictions become sufficiently reliable in
> time to support an engineering decision.
